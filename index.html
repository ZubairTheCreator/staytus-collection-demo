Welcome to Staytus Collection

A Distinctive Approach to Property Development

Staytus Collection is a Stellenbosch-based property development and investment company focused on creating exceptional places and long-term value. Having evolved from a boutique developer into a fully integrated development and investment company, Staytus Collection delivers residential, mixed-use, hospitality, and strategic real estate projects while providing development management services to investors, landowners, and partners. Through disciplined execution, strategic partnerships, and a commitment to quality, Staytus Collection develops, manages, and invests in property assets that generate sustainable returns and lasting impact.

Founders Werner Roux and Handré Basson bring 50 years of combined experience in property development. STAYTUS specialises in revitalizing old and historical properties in Cape Town, Stellenbosch, Paarl, and Somerset West. By focusing on these areas, they ensure efficient project management and better oversight.

In addition to property development, STAYTUS focuses on building lasting relationships with their professional team, financiers, and property partners. Their work is driven by a passion for creating beautiful spaces, which is reflected in each project. Known for their innovative approach, they develop properties and lifestyles that stand the test of time. The newly introduced “Staytus Living” brand highlights their dedication to providing housing for students, young professionals, and more.

Staytus Collection is part of the Rouxcor Group of Companies.

Choose STAYTUS for a development experience that goes beyond the ordinary, where innovation, passion, and a commitment to lasting beauty define every project.

Welcome to a realm where properties and lifestyles are crafted for a lifetime.
